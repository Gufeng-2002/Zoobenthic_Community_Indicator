Decision-region visualization note
===================================

Model: Model B (Core+Peripheral)
Training subset: Core+Peripheral
N training sites: 48
Training accuracy: 77.08%
CV accuracy: 68.89% ± 10.93%

The decision regions shown in the PC1–PC2 plane are a *conditional*
visualization of the full classifier.  The classifier was trained
on the full environmental descriptor space (not on PCA scores).
For every grid point in the PC1–PC2 plane, all omitted PCA
coordinates (PC3 … PC5) are fixed at
their overall-site mean values (computed from all sites, both
reference and non-reference).  The grid PCA-score vectors are
back-transformed into the original environmental space, then
standardised with the classifier's training scaler, and finally
passed to the fitted model for prediction.

Because the omitted PCs are fixed at their means, the map shows
an approximate slice through the full decision surface and may
not capture classification behaviour that is driven primarily by
variability along higher-order PCs.
